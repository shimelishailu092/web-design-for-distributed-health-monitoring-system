About Our System

Smart, Secure & Distributed Health Monitoring

Who We Are

The Distributed Health Monitoring System is an intelligent, web-based healthcare platform designed to continuously monitor patients’ vital signs using wearable sensors and distributed data processing. It enables real-time health tracking, early detection of medical risks, and fast communication between patients and healthcare professionals.

🎯

Our Mission

To improve healthcare delivery by enabling continuous, real-time health monitoring and early detection of critical conditions through secure distributed systems.

🌍

Our Vision

To create accessible, scalable, and reliable digital healthcare solutions that support patients and doctors anytime, anywhere, especially in remote and underserved areas.

How the System Works

⌚

Wearable Sensors

Wearable or simulated sensors continuously measure vital signs such as heart rate, blood pressure, body temperature, and glucose levels.

☁️

Distributed Server

Collected data is securely transmitted to distributed servers where it is stored, analyzed, and validated in real time.

🚨

Alerts & Monitoring

If abnormal health conditions are detected, alerts are automatically generated and sent to doctors and patients for immediate action.

Key Features

📊

Real-Time Monitoring

Live visualization of patient vitals with automatic updates from sensor data.

🔐

Secure & Encrypted

Ensures confidentiality, integrity, and availability of medical data using modern security mechanisms.

👨‍⚕️

Doctor–Patient Collaboration

Enables doctors to monitor patients remotely, review reports, and provide timely medical advice.
